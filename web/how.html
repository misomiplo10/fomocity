How FOMO City works

Buy FOMO anywhere. Every buy adds time to the clock. Whoever bought last when it hits zero wins the jackpot, sent to their wallet automatically.

Read this first. FOMO City is a last-buyer-wins game. Only one wallet wins each round, and everyone else gets nothing from it. Most players lose money. Only spend what you can afford to lose entirely. Nothing here is investment advice.

How to play

  1. Buy FOMO with SOL on any app: Jupiter, Meteora, a wallet's swap screen, a trading bot. There's nothing to buy on this website.
  2. Buy at least for it to count. Smaller buys and all sells are ignored.
  3. Every counted buy adds to the countdown and makes you the last buyer. The clock never shows more than .
  4. When the clock hits zero, the last buyer wins the whole jackpot. It's sent straight to their wallet. There's nothing to claim.
  5. A new round starts right away, with its own fresh countdown.

Watch the city to follow along: every buyer gets a tower that grows with how much they've bought this round, the gold obelisk grows with the jackpot, a beam of light marks the last buyer, and the plaza turns red as the clock runs out.

The rules

  • A buy counts when it puts at least into FOMO's Meteora pool, from any app or aggregator. Sells never count.
  • Each counted buy adds to the countdown, which is capped at . A round lasts if nobody buys, and keeps going as long as people keep buying.
  • When the countdown reaches zero, the last counted buyer wins the entire jackpot.
  • The jackpot is paid automatically to the winner's wallet in the same on-chain step that ends the round.
  • Buys that land after zero don't count for that round. There's a short window after zero so buys made just before it are still recorded before the round closes.
  • Buys are ordered by when they happened on-chain. Buys in the same second are ordered as the game's keeper sees them.
  • The game's own buybacks never count as a player buy.
  • A round with no counted buys carries its whole jackpot into the next round.
  • Recent winners, with the amount they won, are listed on the main page and stored on-chain.

Where the jackpot comes from

FOMO was launched by its creator on Meteora, and its trading fees go to the creator, not to the game. The jackpot is funded by top-ups from the creator and anyone else who wants a bigger prize:

  • SOL sent to the game is used by the keeper to buy FOMO, split like this:

next round's jackpot

  • FOMO sent to the game goes straight into the current round's jackpot (or the next round's, if the clock already hit zero).

Every top-up is recorded on-chain and shown in the live feed.

Where the jackpot comes from

Every trade of FOMO, buy or sell, pays a fee on Meteora. The game's treasury collects 80% of all those fees in SOL (Meteora keeps 20%). A keeper regularly uses that SOL to buy FOMO back, and splits what it buys:

this round's jackpot
next round's jackpot
burned forever

So the jackpot grows with trading, whether or not anyone is playing a round. Buybacks wait until the swap fee is low, and a buyback that lands after the clock hit zero goes to the next round, so it can never change who wins.

The token

  • Launch on a bonding curve. FOMO launches on Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve. The price starts low and rises automatically as people buy.
  • Snipers pay the pot. The trading fee starts at 50% and falls to 1% over the first two minutes. Bots that rush the launch pay heavy fees, and those fees feed the jackpot.
  • Graduation. When the curve has raised its target, FOMO moves to a regular Meteora pool. All of its liquidity is locked forever: nobody, including the team, can pull it. The game keeps earning that pool's fees for the jackpot.
  • No team allocation. No presale, no team tokens, no vesting. Nobody can mint more FOMO.

What you can check, and what you trust

The game runs as an open-source Solana program. Everything below is public on-chain:

  • Every counted buy is recorded with the wallet, the SOL amount, the time and the swap's transaction signature, so anyone can match it to the real trade.
  • The countdown, the last buyer, the jackpot and every payout live in the program's accounts.
  • The jackpot can only be paid to the round's last recorded buyer, and only once.

There are trust points, and you should know them:

  • The keeper reports buys. The program can't see trades made in other apps, so the game's keeper watches Meteora and reports each qualifying buy. It could miss or delay one. Every report is public, so a missing buy is visible to anyone who checks.
  • Meteora can pause the pool after graduation. That would stop buybacks until it resumes; it can't touch the jackpot or the locked liquidity.
  • Program upgrades. Until the program's upgrade authority is revoked, the team could change the program. The goal is to revoke it after launch, and that will be announced and checkable on-chain.

FAQ

Do I need to connect my wallet here?

No. This site only shows the game. You play by buying FOMO wherever you like, and winnings go straight to the wallet that bought.

Does selling remove me as the last buyer?

No. Sells are ignored. Only counted buys change the last buyer.

Does buying more in one go help?

Not for winning: any counted buy makes you the last buyer and adds the same time. Bigger buys only make your tower taller.

What if two people buy in the same second?

The game follows the order the trades landed on-chain. At the very end of a round, expect bots competing for the final second.

When is the jackpot paid?

A few seconds after the clock hits zero, once the short grace window has passed. It's sent to the winning wallet automatically.

Is this gambling?

It's a game of chance with real money at stake. Check whether it's allowed where you live before playing.
